# Hoarding validation plan

## Label definition

Separate event classes: (1) inventory discrepancy in government custody, (2) unlicensed/undeclared private stock, (3) stock legally held but reported during an inquiry, (4) illegal hoarding established by enforcement/adjudication, (5) allegation or seizure pending investigation, and (6) cleared/no violation. Do not collapse those into one binary label.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Final court/tribunal outcome or official adjudicated order with identified grain, quantity, date, and location.
2. Official inspection/seizure record plus case status and corroborating agency record.
3. Police/Food Department/Vigilance release with explicit allegation and recovered quantity, coded as allegation/seizure, not conviction.
4. Credible contemporaneous journalism, retained as a lead until independently confirmed.

The official Tarn Taran district/Vigilance report surfaced in discovery documents a 989-quintal government godown stock discrepancy, inspection, and FIR. It is useful to test the public-custody reconciliation path, but is not evidence of private grain hoarding. Record outcome separately when verified.

## Collection protocol

- Search Punjab India district administration, Punjab Police, Vigilance Bureau, Food/Civil Supplies, courts, and procurement agencies by district, dates, grain form, seizure, stock mismatch, diversion, and hoarding terms.
- Capture primary URLs and archived copies where legally allowed, publication and event dates, named agencies, location precision, quantity/unit, commodity form, legal status, and source wording.
- Have a second reviewer independently verify jurisdiction, event class, unit conversion, and case status. Preserve disagreements and confidence.
- Collect negative examples from inspected facilities with a documented compliant outcome. An absence of news coverage is not a negative label.
- Store exact/approximate coordinates separately with access controls; avoid exposing private parties or sensitive facility coordinates in public products.
- Obtain written data-sharing or lawful public-record access for inspection registers, seizure inventories, stock declarations, warehouse registries, and case dispositions. A newspaper search cannot supply the missing denominator.

## Evaluation design

Use time-separated and location-separated holdouts. Prevent neighboring warehouse/date leakage. Compare a simple seasonal baseline, supply-reconciliation indicators, satellite-only features, and combined indicators. Report precision, recall, false alerts per district-month, calibration, and uncertainty. Due to rare positive events, prioritize precision and field workload in consultation with investigators. Evaluate by commodity and season. Any model output is a lead for review, never a finding of guilt.

## Current status

No curated, independently verified hoarding dataset is present. One official public-stock discrepancy lead was identified; it does not constitute an event dataset. The immediate next action is an evidence-access and case-coding pilot with Punjab Food/Civil Supplies, Pungrain, police/vigilance, and judicial records.
